MIST INSTAGRAM BASE ALBUM
=========================

What's here
- ../index.html ...... open in your browser to see every post and story at a glance
- posts/ ............. 17 grid posts, 1080 x 1350 px (Instagram 4:5)
- stories/ ........... 5 stories, 1080 x 1920 px
- assets/ ............ the logo (gold, dark gold, near-black), illustrations and AI photos

How to edit
1. Right-click a file in posts/ or stories/ > Open with > Notepad (or VS Code).
2. Change the words between the tags, e.g. [FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER] or [@HANDLE].
   Colours are the #codes inside style="...", e.g. #d6b56e is the gold.
3. Save, then double-click the file to see it in your browser.
4. To swap a picture, put your photo in assets/ and change the file name in src="../assets/...".

Turning a post into an image for Instagram
- In Chrome/Edge: open the post, press F12, then Ctrl+Shift+P, type "screenshot"
  and choose "Capture node screenshot" with the .frame element selected,
  or "Capture full size screenshot". That gives a PNG at the exact size.

The live version
- The same album is a design canvas at claude.ai (Mist Instagram Base Album).
  Open it on any computer while signed in to edit it visually.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so open the files with an internet connection.
