# Moody bar photos — AI prompts

Style: like the full-bleed story photo. Photorealistic, dark and warm, one sharp subject up front (a drink, hands, a glass) with the background melting into blur, warm amber bokeh, a little haze or smoke in the light, direct flash or a single warm lamp, film grain. People are only blurred, in shadow or seen from behind, never as recognisable faces.

## Model

**FLUX.1 Krea [dev]** (best for realistic photos)
huggingface.co/spaces/mcp-tools/FLUX.1-Krea-dev

How to use it:
- Sign in to Hugging Face first for more free generation time.
- Set Width and Height under **Advanced Settings** before you press Run. Refreshing the page resets them to 768 × 768.
  - Stories: **720 × 1280**
  - Posts: **896 × 1120**
  - Square, if a size fails: **768 × 768**
- Make 3–4 of each and keep the best.
- If you get "Error", the free time has run out. Wait a while, or try again the next day.

## The style ending

Every prompt below ends with the same text. Copy it onto any new idea to keep the set matching:

```
dark moody cocktail bar, shallow depth of field, warm amber bokeh, soft haze of smoke in the light, direct flash, deep shadows, warm slightly underexposed tones, 35mm film grain, editorial photography
```

## Tips
- **For text over the photo:** add "plenty of dark empty space at the top" (or "at the bottom") so there's room for the date and caption.
- **For one strong colour:** add "deep bottle-green walls" or "oxblood velvet" to match the Mist palette.
- **Sharper photos:** raise "Number of inference steps" under Advanced Settings from 24 to 30–35. Each one takes a bit longer.
- **Keep it in Mist style:** no recognisable faces, no logos or bottle labels (the AI makes fake ones), and no cigars in hands. Smoke in the light is fine.
- **When you have new pictures:** save them in mist_socialmedia/Generated and ask Claude to put them into posts or stories.
